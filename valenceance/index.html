Valenceance

Noun  ·  Coined October 27, 2026  ·  Published October 27, 2026

The deliberate transformation of a choice from something continually compared against alternatives into something whose value is cultivated through commitment.

Comparison discovers value.
Valenceance creates it.
The idea

Value after choosing.

Valenceance describes the transition from searching for the best available option to making a chosen option worthwhile through attention, investment, care, and continued development.

Comparison is useful when making decisions. But perpetual comparison can prevent the very value we hope to discover from ever developing.

Some value is not found. It is created after choosing.

In practice

Where it can take root.

Relationships

Moving beyond perpetual comparison to cultivate a chosen relationship.

Creative work

Selecting an idea or artistic direction and developing its potential rather than endlessly generating alternatives.

Possessions

Choosing something suitable, ending the search, and deriving increasing value through use and care.

Personal philosophy

Recognizing the difference between evaluating possibilities and cultivating reality.

The distinction

What it is not.

  • Blind loyalty or unconditional commitment.
  • Settling for mistreatment or harmful circumstances.
  • Sunk-cost reasoning.
  • Refusing to reconsider a choice when meaningful circumstances change.
Origin · October 27, 2026

A word from a conversation.

Valenceance was developed by Shawn Gilbert in collaboration with ChatGPT during a philosophical conversation on October 27, 2026. It emerged from discussions about comparison, commitment, relationships, and the act of selecting and developing creative work.

It subsequently became the title and central idea of the Tecknow Shaman song Valenceance—an idea carried from conversation into music.
